Frequently Asked Questions

What about payment security?

With any financial product that you buy, it is important that you know you are getting the best advice from a reputable company. Stackly employs end-to-end 256-bit SSL encryption and verified escrow gateways for all freight transactions.

When should I receive my Shipment?

Delivery schedules depend on the selected tier. Standard ground transit takes 2-4 business days, while Priority Air and Emergency Drone deliver within 12 to 24 hours globally.

What happens to my Shipment if I cancel?

Cancellations requested before dispatch are refunded in full. If the cargo is already in transit, our logistics coordinators can redirect the shipment to any regional storage hub.

What types of cargo projects do you handle?

We manage multi-pallet industrial freight, e-commerce direct-to-consumer parcel batches, cold-chain pharmaceuticals, hazardous materials with full hazmat compliance, and oversized machinery.
