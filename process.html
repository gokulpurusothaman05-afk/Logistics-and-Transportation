Our 6-Step Precision Logistics Flow

From the moment you create a booking request to final verified drop-off, discover how our automated infrastructure delivers unparalleled speed and cargo safety.

01

Instant Booking & Cargo Profiling

Submit your origin, destination, weight, dimensions, and speed requirements through our digital portal. Our system instantly classifies goods, assigns customs documentation, and generates a reserved shipping manifest.

02

Automated Route & Carrier Scheduling

Our AI engine analyzes traffic patterns, maritime port congestion, and flight availability to reserve optimal multi-modal legs across ground trucks, container vessels, and cargo aircraft.

03

Pickup & Smart RFID Induction

Stackly couriers collect your consignment from your manufacturing dock or doorstep. Every box is fitted with an encrypted QR/RFID tag for continuous tracking through sorting conveyors.

04

Live Telemetry & In-Transit Monitoring

Throughout transit, IoT sensors beam real-time temperature, humidity, vibration, and exact GPS coordinates to both shipper and receiver via the Stackly Client Dashboard.

05

Regional Sorting & Drone/Van Dispatch

Cargo reaches our local fulfillment node where packages are sorted in under 12 minutes and assigned to clean-electric vans or autonomous aerial delivery drones for the final mile.

06

Verified Delivery & Proof-of-Receipt

The consignment is handed over with contactless cryptographic confirmation or photographic proof. An automated invoice receipt and carbon-offset audit are logged to your dashboard.
